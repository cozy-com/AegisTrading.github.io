# Aegis Trading EA
Product information and documentation for Aegis Trading EA.

## About Aegis Trading

Aegis Trading develops automated trading systems (Expert Advisors) for financial markets, including Forex, Gold, and Bitcoin.

We focus on robust strategy design, extensive backtesting, and practical usability to create reliable EA solutions for different market environments.

## Development Portfolio

Examples of developed EAs:

- Aegis Trend EA
- Aegis Bitcoin Trend
- Aegis Aurora EA
- Aegis XAU EA
- Aegis BTC Balanced EA
- Aegis BTC Aggressive Edition
- Aegis XAU Extreme Edition

Each EA is designed and optimized for specific market characteristics and trading conditions.

## Information

These products are Expert Advisors (EAs) exclusively designed for MetaTrader 5 (MT5).

Please click the EA widget to access and use the products through the MQL5 website.

New products will be released progressively after testing and completion.
